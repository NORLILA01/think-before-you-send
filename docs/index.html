How this works and its limits

A small classifier trained on public comment data (Civil Comments, CC0) scores your text locally. It misses roughly half of aggressive messages and can flag harmless ones, so treat it as a prompt to reread, not a verdict.

If you choose AI coaching, only that message is sent to a proxy and on to a third-party language model (via OpenRouter). It is not stored by this site. Do not include names, passwords or other private details.

This is a communication aid, not therapy or crisis support.
